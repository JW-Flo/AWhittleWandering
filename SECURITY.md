# Security Policy

Primary Security Contact

- Email: <security@atlasit.pro> (preferred alias)
- Direct (fallback): <joe.whittle@atlasit.pro>

Reporting Guidelines

- Report vulnerabilities privately via email above.
- Include: affected component, reproduction steps, observed vs expected behavior, potential impact, suggested remediation (optional).
- Do not publicly disclose prior to coordinated fix & advisory.
- For critical issues, use subject prefix: [CRITICAL].

Response Commitments

- Acknowledge receipt within 2 business days.
- Provide initial triage classification within 5 business days.
- Target fix or mitigation ETA communicated in first response.

Out of Scope (Examples)

- Denial of service via unrealistic resource exhaustion not reproducible in production config.
- Self XSS requiring user to paste code in console.

Temporary Safe Harbor
Good-faith research following these guidelines will not result in legal action. Avoid accessing other tenants' data or performing destructive tests.
